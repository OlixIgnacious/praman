# YES Bank — RBI Divergence Disclosures (FY18, FY19)

Two linked events, both usable as Stage 2 eval ground truth ("bank reported X, RBI's Risk Assessment Report found Y").

## FY19 — ₹3,277 crore divergence (primary candidate)
- Bank had classified ₹1,259 crore as NPA as of Sept 30, 2019, with ₹346 crore provisions.
- RBI's Final Risk Assessment Report found incremental gross NPA of ₹2,018 crore, requiring additional provisioning of ₹632 crore.
- Total divergence disclosed: ₹3,277 crore, published per SEBI's 24-hour disclosure mandate.
- Source: https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/finance/rbi-found-rs-3-277-cr-divergence-in-npa-provisioning-for-fy19-yes-bank-119111901799_1.html

## FY18 — "Nil divergence" controversy (bonus: a governance/disclosure-integrity angle)
- YES Bank initially disclosed a "nil divergence" RAR outcome for FY18; stock rallied ~32% same day.
- RBI subsequently stated the bank's disclosure of only the "nil divergence" finding, while omitting other lapses/regulatory breaches noted in the same RAR, was a deliberate attempt to mislead the public, and threatened action for violating RAR confidentiality.
